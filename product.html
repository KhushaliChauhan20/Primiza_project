Plastic Storage Box
This is a modular plastic storage box designed for home organization. It features a front-opening double door with orange handles for easy access, even when stacked. The semi-transparent smoked plastic body allows visibility of the contents without needing to open the box. The box includes side handles for convenient lifting and a flat, reinforced top for stable stacking. Suitable for storing clothes, toys, accessories, kitchen items, tools, and more.
